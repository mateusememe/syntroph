The biology, in short

Why syntrophy

In biology, syntrophy is symbiosis at its most economical: one organism's metabolic output is another's fuel, and nothing is wasted.

Syntroph mirrors that trade. Every adapter's output feeds the next through the event bus, so no session starts from zero.

How it's built

A hexagon at the core.

At the center sits a single event bus — the metabolic exchange through which every signal flows.

Around it, seven ports define the contract each domain must satisfy.

Adapters are swappable organisms that plug into those ports: change an adapter, keep the port, keep the system.

Quickstart

terminal — syntroph
$ syntroph clone github.com/your-org/your-repo
$ syntroph config
# adapters can be toggled interactively

Everything is configurable via .syntroph/config.yaml — adapters can be enabled or disabled individually.
